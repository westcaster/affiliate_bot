Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra Review: The Pinnacle of Automated Floor Care

When you drop nearly two grand on a household appliance, standard cleaning performance simply does not cut it. You expect near-autonomous perfection, flawless navigation, and absolute reliability. The Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra arrives with a staggering $1,799.99 price tag and an extensive feature list designed to justify every single dollar.

At the center of this system is the 8-in-1 RockDock Ultra—a maintenance hub that washes, dries, empties, and refills the robot automatically. Paired with 10,000 Pa of suction, a mechanical extending side brush, and a pivoting mop arm, this machine targets the toughest blind spots in modern homes. This buyer’s guide breaks down how the S8 MaxV Ultra performs under everyday conditions, putting its engineering claims to the test.

Quick Verdict

Rating: 4.8 / 5.0

The Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra represents the current ceiling of robotic floor care. It combines raw power with sophisticated mechanical extensions to clean where other robots cannot physically reach. While the investment is substantial, the reduction in manual maintenance makes it worth every penny for busy households.

Key Takeaways:

  • Unmatched Edge Cleaning: The extendable FlexiArm side brush and mop sweep baseboards and tuck into tight 90-degree corners with physical precision.
  • True Hot Water Maintenance: The RockDock Ultra cleans mop pads with 140°F hot water, preventing the sour odors typical of damp microfiber in enclosed docks.
  • Exceptional Obstacle Avoidance: Reactive AI 2.0 uses an onboard camera and structured light to actively identify and sidestep phone cords, pet waste, and stray socks.
  • High Financial Barrier: At $1,799.99, it sits firmly in the luxury appliance tier, pricing out budget-conscious homeowners.

In-Depth Engineering & Real-World Usability Breakdown

HyperForce Suction and the DuoRoller Riser

At peak operation, the S8 MaxV Ultra churns out 10,000 Pa of HyperForce suction. In daily testing, this translates to effortless extraction of fine sand trapped deep within medium-pile wool rugs and heavy debris pulled cleanly from textured hardwood grout lines.

Roborock pairs this suction with dual all-rubber rollers rotating in opposite directions. Unlike bristle brushes that wind hair into tight knots requiring scissors to cut away, the counter-rotating rubber blades agitate fibers and feed hair directly toward the suction inlet. Furthermore, the entire roller assembly automatically elevates when the machine transitions to mop-only mode or returns to the dock, preventing wet pads from dragging across clean carpets.

FlexiArm Design: Mechanical Extension for Edges

Most traditional robot vacuums leave a dead zone of about one to two inches along baseboards because their circular frames cannot get flush against perpendicular walls. Roborock solves this structural limitation on the S8 MaxV Ultra via the FlexiArm Design.

When the robot senses an edge or a 90-degree corner, the physical side brush swings outward on a hinged arm to sweep debris out from the wall base. Simultaneously, the VibraRise 3.0 mopping module physically extends its right-side pad outward to scrub flush against baseboards. In practical use, this mechanical reach eliminates the tedious weekly chore of running a manual handheld vacuum along hallway edges.

RockDock Ultra: Automated Maintenance

The 8-in-1 RockDock Ultra station is a heavy-duty command center finished in matte black plastics that resist fingerprint smudges. Inside the dock, separate clean and dirty water tanks manage automated mop washing and fresh tank refills.

The standout hardware feature here is the 140°F hot water wash cycle. Cold water struggles to dissolve dried kitchen oils and stubborn residue absorbed by microfiber pads. By running hot water over the pads during the cleaning cycle, the dock breaks down grease effectively. This is followed by a heated air-drying cycle that blows warm air across the damp pads, inhibiting mold growth and eliminating sour smells over extended periods of use.

Navigation and Onboard AI

Navigation relies on advanced LiDAR mapping paired with Reactive AI 2.0 obstacle recognition. The front-mounted RGB camera and structured light projector allow the robot to identify over 70 distinct household objects—including scales, power strips, and pet waste.

During test runs in cluttered living spaces, the S8 MaxV Ultra actively reroutes around obstacles instead of simply bumping into them blindly. Through the companion app, the robot snaps reference photos of unidentified items, letting you confirm whether it is a permanent fixture or temporary clutter to be picked up later. The "Hello Rocky" voice assistant also lets you issue direct verbal commands without reaching for your smartphone.

Search-Optimized FAQ

Does the Roborock S8 MaxV Ultra work well on high-pile carpets?

Yes. The 10,000 Pa suction level pulls embedded dirt from thick carpets easily, and the dual rubber rollers lift to prevent the mopping pad from dampening carpet fibers during cleaning routines.

How often do the water tanks need to be manually emptied and refilled?

Under normal daily operation in a 2,000-square-foot home, the clean water tank in the RockDock Ultra lasts roughly 4 to 6 days before needing a refill, while the dirty water tank requires emptying on a similar schedule to prevent stagnation.

Can the camera on the S8 MaxV Ultra be used for home security?

Yes, the onboard RGB camera supports remote viewing and two-way audio communication through the app, allowing you to check in on pets or verify that your home is secure while you are away.
